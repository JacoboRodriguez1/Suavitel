\section{Evaluation and Results}
\label{sec:evaluation}

Among the three neural architectures, Dense achieved the highest mean test
macro F1 (0.741), followed by LSTM (0.701) and SimpleRNN (0.551).
Table~\ref{tab:neural-results} compares them with a stratified dummy that
samples labels using the training class proportions. This baseline was
evaluated on the same five seeds. Neural predictions use a threshold of
0.5. Precision, recall and F1 refer to the positive class, while macro F1
weights both classes equally.

Dense fell from 0.990 training macro F1 to 0.741 on test. Despite this gap,
its order-insensitive encoder outperformed both recurrent alternatives on
these short comments. LSTM had the smallest test macro F1 standard deviation
among the neural models (0.021). The variation reflects initialization and
training order with the same 200 test comments in every run.

Table~\ref{tab:neural-confusion} shows why the recurrent models differ.
LSTM reduced false positives from 61 to 23 relative to SimpleRNN, but
missed 34 positive comments versus 21. On the positive comment
\emph{I didn't find it too bad}, SimpleRNN recovered the approval label and
LSTM predicted negative. Both models missed a negative comment that used
\emph{good} in an apparently ironic question. Cleaning retained the word
and removed its quotation marks.
